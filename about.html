Our Story

Built on strong values, growing with the times

Morning Star Higher Secondary School was established with a simple but ambitious goal: to provide world-class, values-driven education accessible to every child in our community. Over two decades, that goal has grown into a full-fledged higher secondary institution.

Today we serve students from Nursery through Class XII, following the CBSE curriculum and the National Education Policy 2020 framework, with an English-medium, co-educational campus.
