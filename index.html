About Morning Star

Two decades of nurturing curious, capable minds

Founded with the mission of delivering world-class education in a nurturing environment, Morning Star Higher Secondary School has grown into one of the region's most trusted higher secondary schools.

Our classrooms blend modern pedagogy with strong fundamentals, preparing students for board examinations as well as competitive entrance tests like JEE and NEET.
