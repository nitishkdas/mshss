Curriculum

A future-ready academic journey

Our curriculum is designed around the NEP 2020 framework and SEBA guidelines, structured across four stages to match each child's developmental needs.

🧸

Kindergarten

Nursery – UKG. Play-based learning focused on motor, language and social development.

📘

Primary

Class I – V. Strong foundations in literacy, numeracy and inquiry-based learning.

📗

Middle & Secondary

Class VI – X. Subject specialisation begins, with labs, projects and board exam preparation.

🎓

Senior Secondary

Class XI – XII. Science, Commerce and Humanities streams with JEE/NEET preparation support.

Beyond the Textbook

Preparing students for competitive futures

Alongside the core SEBA curriculum, Morning Star Higher Secondary School offers structured support for national-level competitive examinations and additional language learning.

  • Integrated JEE & NEET preparation for Science stream students
  • French language classes from Middle School onward
  • Regular assessments, remedial classes and doubt-clearing sessions
  • Career counselling and stream-selection guidance from Class IX
